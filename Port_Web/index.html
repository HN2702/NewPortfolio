About

Software engineering graduate, IT engineer by day, product builder the rest of the time.

I work as an IT Engineer at Punjab College, where most of my time goes into keeping systems running for people who need them to just work. Outside of that I build products end to end — a hybrid desktop and cloud POS system with FBR digital invoicing, Flutter apps, and the storefront for a small e-commerce business I run myself.

Running my own store taught me more about product decisions than any tutorial did: if checkout is confusing, nobody buys. That's the lens I bring to interfaces — clarity first, then polish.

I'm most useful on projects where the requirements are still moving and someone needs to turn a rough idea into something running.
